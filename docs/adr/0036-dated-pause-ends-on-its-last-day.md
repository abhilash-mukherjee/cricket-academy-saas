# Dated pause ends on its last paused day

A dated pause has a last paused day. The Enrollment stays paused through that day, is excluded from Session attendance, and is Active again the next day. valid-until grows by every paused day. No resume button and no scheduled job. The Owner may resume earlier: paused days then run from the start through the day before resume, and the planned last day is discarded. Pausing and resuming on the same day adds no days. An open-ended pause has no last day and waits for an explicit resume. The first paused day is on or after the Enrollment’s valid-from, and today or earlier, including for an open-ended pause. A dated last day may fall after valid-until. While a pause is open, no other Enrollment may be created for that Player and Batch. A dated pause saved with its last day already in the past extends valid-until on that save. A read computes a finished dated pause (resumed state and extended valid-until) and does not write. The next Owner command that writes in a transaction persists that settlement before it decides.

**Considered options:** A reminder date that never ends the pause (rejected — the typed day does not cap the extension, and attendance stays blocked until someone presses Resume); typing the interval only after the absence (rejected — exclusion has to hold while the Player is away, and a saved Session list does not change afterward).
