# Three jobs, two looks: Academy template vs product chrome

Academy brochure and conversion pages share one public template (Academy-first, marketing density). Everything else — product homepage, auth, Owner/Coach `/app/…`, and Super-admin `/app/admin/…` — shares the product chrome (same brand tokens, compact density on staff screens). Owner and Coach share Academy ops chrome; Coach is a subset of nav. Super-admin is a platform console (list of Academies), not an empty Owner inbox; impersonation borrows the Owner shell plus the banner in ADR 0018. One visual system with two densities beats three design systems or a Brochure that looks like an admin tool. Footer “built with” credit on public Academy pages is a product copy choice, not this decision.
